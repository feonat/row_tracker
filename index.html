Stitch reference

Make bobble (MB)

  1. Knit into the front, back and front again of the stitch (3 sts from 1).
  2. Turn to WS, p3.
  3. Turn to RS, k3.
  4. Turn to WS, p3.
  5. Turn to RS, slip 2, k1, pass the slipped sts over.

Mirror after the middle stitch

k2tog before the middle stitch becomes skp after it. Row starts and ends mirror too.

Abbreviations

k knit · p purl · yo yarn over · k2tog knit 2 together · skp slip 1, k1, pass slipped st over · m marker · pm place marker · slm slip marker · M1L / M1R make one left / right · BO bind off
